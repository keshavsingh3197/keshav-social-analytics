## 1. Core Insight: Why Long-Form (16:9) Drives Earnings & YPP Monetization

- **YPP Eligibility Rule:** Only **16:9 public long-form VODs and Live Streams** count toward the **4,000 valid public watch hours** required for the YouTube Partner Program (Shorts watch hours are tracked separately and do not count toward the 4,000h threshold).
- **Higher RPM & Ad Revenue:** Long-form 16:9 videos (especially 8+ min with mid-roll eligibility) generate significantly higher AdSense RPM than Shorts.
- **Stronger Subscriber Conversion:** On `OMKAR SHIV Bhakti`, longer videos convert **12.2 subscribers per 1,000 views** vs **3.8 subscribers per 1,000 views** for Shorts (~3.2× higher).
